🔬 Forensic Evidence Acquisition

Workflow: acquire on an authorized forensic workstation → preserve the source → calculate SHA-256 → record the acquisition metadata → upload the forensic image/export. The web app does not bypass device locks or directly mount raw phones/USB devices.

Examples: forensic image, logical extraction, exported logs, memory capture, documents. Large physical images should be acquired/stored with a dedicated forensic workstation and evidence storage.
